Bridge Pay — Bitcoin Lightning Payment Widget

Bridge Pay is a plug-and-play Lightning payment widget that lets merchants accept Bitcoin without any technical overhead. Once installed, the system handles everything automatically — converting fiat prices to satoshis in real time at the moment of purchase, so merchants never have to think about volatility and customers always pay a fair amount.

The goal: Remove every friction point between a merchant and Bitcoin acceptance. A customer seeing a familiar dollar price that automatically settles in sats is far more likely to pay with Lightning than one staring at a raw BTC amount.

Key Capabilities

  • Real-time BTC/fiat conversion at point of sale — prices reflect the exact exchange rate at checkout
  • Split payment support for flexible transaction handling
  • Compatible with any Lightning-enabled wallet
  • Native WooCommerce integration, with Shopify support in progress
  • Xero accounting integration for seamless bookkeeping
  • Zero ongoing management required after deployment

Currently at working demo stage.
